Every project.
One source of truth.

Track the services, costs, and credentials behind everything you build. Data stays on your device — and in your iCloud account only if you turn sync on.

No TechShelf account. There is no TechShelf server that receives your projects, secrets, or costs.

Encrypted credentials

Secret values are AES-256 encrypted on the device before they are stored. The key lives in Keychain. Face ID, if enabled, only unlocks the Credentials tab.

Optional iCloud

CloudKit sync is yours to turn on. When it is off, nothing leaves the phone. Deleting the app removes on-device data.

Local MCP only

AI Assistant Access runs on your Wi-Fi while TechShelf is open. Pairing tokens stay on the device. MCP tools cannot read secret values.
